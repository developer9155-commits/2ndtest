HealthGIS

A mapping platform that brings health information onto interactive maps.

The idea

Health numbers are easier to understand when you can see where they happen. HealthGIS puts data on maps and dashboards, so patterns by area become easy to spot and decisions become clearer.

Planned capabilities

  • Map layers that show health information by location.
  • Clear charts and dashboards anyone can read.
  • Verified data coordinated with partner organisations.

Concept in development

CropIndia AI

An agricultural intelligence application concept for crop production information and visualization.

The idea

India's crop data is spread across many sources and is hard to read at a glance. CropIndia AI is planned as one place where students, farmers and researchers can look up crop production information and see it as clear visuals.

Planned capabilities

  • Look up crop production information.
  • Visualize that information with charts and maps.
  • Use AI to explain the data in plain language.

None of these features are live yet. This section describes the plan, not a finished product.
